## Claim
Institutions fell from 0.83 to 0.40 while debt hit the cap and the income floor collapsed from 0.67 to 0.16, output per person from 9.7× to 2×, unemployment to 26%, stability to 0.13. The gerontocracy rule was reading a chronological median age of 95 (life expectancy 130) as extreme old age and decaying institutions and capital every month.
## What it constrains in the model
Gerontocracy uses biological age: median age minus 0.6 × years of life expectancy above 80. A polity of healthy 95-year-olds is not a gerontocracy.
## How we would know it is wrong
The same slide reappearing in the 300-year Inhabit Everything run with the fix in place.
