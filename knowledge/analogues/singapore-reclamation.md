## Claim
581.5 km² in 1960 to about 736 km² in 2019, roughly +25%. Shallow fill costs US$10–40/m²; deep or hard sites US$400+/m²; sand volumes 1.5–37.5 million m³ per km². Reclaimed land subsides 1–3 cm/yr on fill; airports on fill (Incheon, Pudong) 12–28 cm/yr.
## Where it comes from
Government history pages, industry cost surveys, trade press on the polder method (halves sand volume, needs permanent pumps).
## What it constrains in the model
Reclamation is a national multi-decade programme, cheap on the shelf and 4–10× dearer beyond it; adding ~10–25% of a small state's land in a century is the ceiling; permanent maintenance and exposure to sea level are part of the price.
## How we would know it is wrong
If any civilization in a 100-year run reclaims more than 30% of its 2026 land, or does it while land pressure is under 0.9.
