## Claim
Large unconditional transfers raise births modestly (order +0.1 to +0.2 in the total fertility rate), part of it timing; targeted pronatal packages do more at a high cost per extra birth.
## What it constrains in the model
The income floor moves the family-formation norm by up to 0.05 (about +0.16 in fertility), down from 0.07.
## How we would know it is wrong
Fertility rising by more than 0.3 within a decade of a floor, with no other change.
