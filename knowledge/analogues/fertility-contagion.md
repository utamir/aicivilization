## Claim
Brazil's fertility fell with the reach of telenovelas; Iran's fell from ~6 to ~2 in fifteen years with education and media; Israel keeps fertility near 3 at OECD income through cohesive communities. Norms diffuse through media, education and trade contact; cohesion resists.
## What it constrains in the model
A civilization's fertility target drifts toward its partners' through trade and science ties, weighted by openness, at a rate that closes about half the gap in a generation at full exposure, multiplied by (1 − culturalCohesion).
## How we would know it is wrong
If open, connected polities in the simulation keep fertility 0.8 above their partners for a century.
