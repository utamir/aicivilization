# Mechanism spec: <name>

Written by the architect from one or more knowledge notes. An implementer builds from this and nothing else.

1. Purpose: the behaviour the world lacks, in one sentence, and the notes that justify it (ids).
2. State: new or changed fields in `types.ts`, with units and 2026 initial values per civilization.
3. Rules: what depends on what, as sentences with constants, in the order the monthly step will apply them. Which existing rules it reads; which it may write.
4. Constants: each with its note id, value, plausible range, and what breaks outside the range.
5. Triggers and choices: chronicle events (title pattern, category, significance, causes, counterforces); decision card if a choice matters (question, options, consequences, affinities).
6. Visible states: what must be seen in the 3D world and on the cards, including the "why" text.
7. Tests: invariants to add; reference numbers expected in seed 7 for baseline and for one stress preset.
8. Non-goals: what this mechanism deliberately does not do, so nobody extends it by accident.
9. Ownership: file(s), the agent, the reviewer.
