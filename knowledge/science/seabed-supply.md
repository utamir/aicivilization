## Claim
Nodule fields hold multiples of land reserves for nickel, cobalt and manganese. A single large project adds ~10% to world manganese supply, which caps the economics; cobalt demand may peak by 2040. Realistic supply share by 2065: a third, not a majority.
## What it constrains in the model
Seabed inflow ceiling 40% of 2026 demand, fields fade 1.2%/yr, waste and backlash per unit.
## How we would know it is wrong
Seabed supply above 45% of demand in any run, or commercial nodule output exceeding 10% of world nickel before 2040.
