## Claim
Roughly a 2% annual chance of a pandemic of COVID scale, rising with urbanization, travel and land-use change; five influenza-class pandemics in a century; mortality concentrated above 65 by a factor of 3–10.
## What it constrains in the model
Base hazard 2.5%/yr scaled by urbanization, trade and warming, divided by biotech capability; deaths weighted 3× in the 65+ bands; lockdown trades output for lives.
## How we would know it is wrong
More than two COVID-scale events per 50 years in baseline, or none in 200.
