## Claim
Fission construction cost fell ~10% per doubling in Korea and rose in real terms in France and the US. Large, long-lived, site-specific plants learn slowly; learning needs standardized designs, stable regulation and a continuous order book.
## What it constrains in the model
Fusion cost falls 8% per doubling of the operating fleet, slower with regulatory caution, not at all where institutions are weak. The earlier 10% per plant was a granular-technology rate misapplied.
## How we would know it is wrong
If SPARC/ARC-class plants show per-unit cost declines above 15% between consecutive units.
