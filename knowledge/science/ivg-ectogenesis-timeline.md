## Claim
Mouse offspring from stem-cell-derived gametes exist (2023). Human IVG is estimated at 2035–2045 by the labs involved. Partial ectogenesis for extreme preterm infants is in regulatory discussion; full ectogenesis from embryo has no timeline.
## What it constrains in the model
IVG is a mid-century biotech invention; full ectogenesis later and harder; a synthetic-birth programme is a policy on top of both, with child-rearing capacity and trust as the binding costs, not the biology.
## How we would know it is wrong
Human IVG births before 2035 (too pessimistic) or none by 2060 (too optimistic) in the world; in the model, synthetic births appearing while fertility is above replacement.
