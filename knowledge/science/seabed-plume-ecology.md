## Claim
Ploughed nodule fields show no meaningful faunal recovery after 26 years; plumes reduce filter-feeder density far beyond the mined strip; nodule-dependent species have no habitat to return to.
## What it constrains in the model
Waste accumulation per unit of seabed inflow doubled (0.05/yr), backlash doubled; supply fading per field unchanged.
## How we would know it is wrong
Recovery reported at DISCOL or CCZ trial sites within a decade.
