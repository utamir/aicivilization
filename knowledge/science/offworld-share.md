## Claim
The largest sustained emigrations in history moved a third to a half of a population over two generations, under famine or land hunger, to destinations that were cheaper to reach than orbit will be for a long time.
## What it constrains in the model
Off-world population capped at 60% of a polity, 85% when habitat capacity exceeds twice its population; migration follows crowding, land pressure and instability. Speculative and marked as such on the Frontier tab.
## How we would know it is wrong
Nothing observable yet; the note exists so nobody mistakes the cap for knowledge.
