## Claim
When childbearing is postponed, period fertility falls more than completed cohort fertility; recuperation at later ages recovers roughly a third to a half of the postponed births. A longer healthy life would extend the window, but the same forces that extend it (education, careers, security) postpone further.
## What it constrains in the model
The longevity bonus on the fertility target is capped at +0.35 and starts at LE 88, replacing +0.55 from LE 85.
## How we would know it is wrong
If long-lived cohorts in the simulation exceed replacement fertility purely from the window effect, or if the real first slowed-aging cohorts show completed fertility above 2.
