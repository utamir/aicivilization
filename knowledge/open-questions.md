# Open questions and modelling judgments

Release review: 2026-09-09. This file deliberately does **not** call uncertainty “answered” merely because the simulator needs a number. Each item is classified as evidence-constrained, a modelling judgment, or genuinely speculative.

1. **Fertility under longevity — evidence-constrained, still uncertain.** Longer healthy life can move timing and the feasible reproductive window without implying a proportional rise in completed fertility. The model caps the longevity fertility bonus and lets policy/culture/housing/technology move fertility separately. See `science-longevity-fertility`.
2. **Fusion learning — evidence-constrained, contested.** Historical nuclear construction does not support a universal learning curve. The model applies learning at fleet scale and makes it conditional on institutional/regulatory execution rather than awarding cheap fusion automatically. See `science-fusion-learning`.
3. **Off-world habitat cost floor and migration share — speculative.** There is no empirical population-scale settlement dataset. Capacity, migration appetite and deep-space residence are therefore scenario mechanics, not forecasts. See `science-offworld-share`.
4. **Fertility contagion — evidence-constrained reduced form.** Connected populations can converge in fertility norms, but the rate and mechanism are context-dependent. The model uses trade/science openness and cultural cohesion as a transparent approximation. See `analogue-fertility-contagion`.
5. **Seabed mineral contribution — conditional and policy-sensitive.** The model permits seabed supply but caps it and charges ecological/social costs. Long-lived ecological disturbance is supported; commercial-scale future contribution remains uncertain. See `science-seabed-supply` and `science-seabed-plume-ecology`.
6. **AI capability / “AGI” arrival — unresolved by design.** There is no defensible single empirical calendar date. METR warns that its present time-horizon suite is unreliable above roughly 16 hours, while the 2026 International AI Safety Report treats stall, slowdown, continuation and acceleration as all plausible by 2030. The UI therefore caps benchmark-like task-horizon display at its evidence range and uses a separate dimensionless deep-future autonomy variable. AGI/ASI are model-defined regimes gated by capability, reliability, compute, institutions and scenario conditions; they are not benchmark-derived dates. See `science-agi-timeline`.
7. **Institutional decline in very old populations — modelling judgment.** Earlier versions accidentally used chronological age as a proxy for institutional gerontocracy under radical longevity. The current model uses biological/functional age instead. This remains a deliberately simplified social mechanism, not a law of aging societies. See `analogue-nemea-slide`.
8. **Output in a post-embodied economy — modelling judgment.** GDP-like output becomes conceptually weak when many persons are digital or off-world. The model now avoids counting digital residents twice: total population already includes them, so digital substrate changes productivity through compute adequacy rather than adding a second labor pool. Orbital industry adds a bounded service/productivity term. These are accounting conventions for scenario comparison, not empirical estimates of a post-biological economy. See `science-output-post-embodied`.
9. **Engineered-pandemic hazard — contested stress-test assumption.** Forecasting Research Institute experts estimated a 0.3% median annual baseline risk of a *human-caused epidemic causing 100,000 deaths*, rising to 1.5% under a specified hypothetical LLM capability and falling to 0.4% with safeguards/screening. That endpoint is not “civilizational pandemic” or extinction risk. The simulator's engineered-hazard term is therefore explicitly a stress-test mapping whose scale should be revisited as evidence changes. See `science-engineered-pandemic`.
10. **Deep-sea ecological recovery — evidence-constrained direction, uncertain magnitude.** The DISCOL experiment still showed biological effects after 26 years, including substantially reduced suspension feeders. That supports persistent local damage, not a universal claim of irreversible ocean collapse. See `science-seabed-plume-ecology`.
11. **Income support and fertility — evidence-constrained, small effect.** The model keeps the effect modest and secondary to norms, housing, timing, opportunity cost and reproductive technology. See `analogue-income-floor-fertility`.
12. **Autonomous machine civilization after humans — intentionally incomplete.** Autonomous infrastructure can maintain explicitly represented systems, but the reference model does not yet implement a fully independent machine civilization that reproduces its own industrial base indefinitely after all biological/digital persons are gone. Zero total persons therefore remains literal extinction in the current ontology.

## Rule for future agents

Do not close an open question because a scenario needs a parameter. Research it, record the evidence class, choose a bounded assumption when necessary, and make the uncertainty inspectable. A reproducible modelling judgment is acceptable. Fake certainty is not.
